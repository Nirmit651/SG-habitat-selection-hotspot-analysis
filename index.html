What does it mean?

Selection indices describe where observations were recorded, not proof that individual deer prefer a habitat. Survey effort and detectability may vary.

How to interpret the flows

Changes in active-polygon totals indicate how recorded activity is distributed across habitat classes. They do not prove that the same physical location gained or lost deer.

An exact polygon-to-polygon alluvial diagram and geolocation map require stable geographic identifiers or GIS geometries. The 2025 source uses a different ID field, so cross-year location matching remains unverified.
